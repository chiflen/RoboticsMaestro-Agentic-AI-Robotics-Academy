# Level 2 Capstone — Grounded Operations Agent
Build a RAG + tool workflow for a simulated organization. It retrieves policy facts, qualifies a request and prepares a proposed action for human approval.

Must have: source-aware retrieval; two validated tools; explicit states and timeout handling; human approval for external/consequential actions; 20 evaluation cases; prompt-injection test; privacy-aware logs; portable provider adapter or decision record.

Robotics option: diagnose simulated telemetry; do not control physical hardware. Demo baseline, metrics and remaining failure modes.
