# Level 1 Capstone — AI Community Helper
Build a small assistant over approved documents that answers with evidence and admits when it does not know.

Must have: CLI or simple UI; documented scope; one deterministic helper; 10 test questions including unanswerable cases; malformed-input handling; README, limitations and test results.

Stretch: simulated robot status panel or state-machine visualization. Demo: problem, happy path, failure case, tests and next step.
