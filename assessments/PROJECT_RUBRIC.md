# Project Rubric (100 points)
- Problem definition 10
- Functionality 15
- Tests/evaluation 15
- Architecture 10
- Security/privacy 15
- Reliability/recovery 10
- UX/accessibility 5
- Documentation 5
- Demo/technical defense 5
- Reflection/impact 10

Score each dimension 0–4 and multiply by weight/4. Critical safety, privacy or secret-management failures cap the score until fixed.
