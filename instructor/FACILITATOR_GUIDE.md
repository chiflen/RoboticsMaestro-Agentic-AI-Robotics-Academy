# Facilitator Guide
## Before cohort
Publish schedule, prerequisites, conduct and accessibility channel; test links; prepare cloud-free fallback; create repo template; use synthetic data; explain learner accountability for AI-generated code.

## During class
Start from a user problem. Demonstrate one failure. Ask learners to predict outputs. Use rotating pair-programming roles: driver, reviewer, tester and documenter. Require test/evidence at checkpoints. Review privacy and tool permissions before connecting integrations.

## Coaching prompts
- What evidence would change your mind?
- What should be deterministic?
- What is the minimum permission required?
- How will you measure improvement?
- Can the result be reproduced without the original chat?
- What is the fallback if the provider fails?

## Integrity
AI tools may be used when disclosed and defended. Do not fabricate tests, citations, benchmarks or contributions. Use oral defense and commit history to assess understanding.
